**One-sentence pitch:** While Claude is in control, game time passes only inside a run
budget Claude asks for, and anything dangerous revokes it and pauses the game.

## Core loop
- A batch arms the governor; `run <days>` / `run until arrived|done|event [max <d>]` grants a budget (a goal with no `run` gets an implicit `run until done`, max `gov_implicit_max_days`).
- Every 0.5 s the governor checks supplies, fuel, CR, threats and incidents; a new alarm revokes the budget, pauses (through `modPause()`) and logs an ALERT.
- Claude reads the alert, fixes or accepts it, and sends `run` again, which acknowledges current alarms.
- With the player in control, the same alarms only warn; nothing pauses.

## Player goal & fail state
- Working: time never passes without a budget; the player's own pause is never undone; every danger the checks cover stops the game once, in time to act.
- Fail: time passes unbudgeted, the mod unpauses a player's pause, or the fleet runs out of fuel or supplies, or is caught by a stronger fleet, without an alarm first.

## MVP — what the system must do
- Run budget: grant (0–365 days), spend only while unpaused, pause when used up or the `until` condition is met.
- Enforcement: armed, no budget, no dialog or battle → paused every frame.
- Player-pause hold (1.0.9): a pause the mod didn't make holds until the player unpauses or a new `run`; a save that loads paused doesn't count; never unpause while a battle is open in a dialog.
- Alarms and latch: supply tiers (under min, min/2, min/4, empty), fuel to the nearest friendly market with margin, lowest combat CR, closing stronger hostile, ships lost or hull lost outside battle; each fires once until acknowledged, cleared or worse; incidents always fire.
- Routine alarms (1.0.5): supply/fuel while heading to a market, and hull scrapes under `gov_incident_pause_pct`, warn without pausing.

## Out of scope — not building this
- The governor doesn't fix problems (no buying, no routing); goals and Claude do.
- No unpausing a player's pause, ever.
